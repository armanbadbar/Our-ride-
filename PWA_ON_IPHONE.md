# Our Ride — iPhone PWA

1. Host this folder on any HTTPS static hosting service.
2. Open the site in Safari on iPhone.
3. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
4. Open the Our Ride icon from the Home Screen.

The app stores demo rides and UPI ID locally on the device. Real OTP, GPS/maps, driver matching, notifications, backend and production payments still require server/API integrations.
